# Subscriptions and Billing

## Plans Overview

### Lite (Free Forever)
- Manage Credits
- Update Profile Page
- Access Company Page
- Notifications
- No credit card required

### Pro ($10-12.50/month)
- Everything in Lite
- Profile Analytics (data across 7 platforms: Spotify, YouTube, YouTube Music, Shazam, TikTok, SoundCloud, Instagram)
- Catalog Audits (MLC gap detection, ISRC-to-ISWC matching)
- Discography Playlists (AI-curated Spotify playlist)
- Milestones & Charts
- 1 Pro Seat included
- Additional seats: $7.50/month each

### Business ($24-30/month starting)
- Everything in Pro
- Company Analytics (aggregate stats for studios, labels, publishers)
- List Analytics (track custom lists of profiles)
- Multi-User Workspace (team collaboration with role-based access)
- Discovery Tools (find talent by stream count, genre, independence status)
- 5 Pro Seats included
- 3 Users included

## Seats and Users

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Seat** | A profile slot -- determines how many profiles you can track analytics for |
| **User** | A team member account -- determines how many people can access the workspace |
| **Profile Limit** | Maximum profiles a subscription can track analytics for |
| **User Limit** | Maximum team members who can access a workspace |

### Seat Counts by Plan
- **Pro**: 1 seat (expandable at $7.50/month per additional seat)
- **Business**: 5 seats included (expandable with higher roster tiers)

## Common Billing Questions

### How do I upgrade from Lite to Pro?
Log in at app.muso.ai, go to your account settings, and select the Pro plan. You can start with a monthly subscription.

### How do I upgrade from Pro to Business?
Go to account settings and select the Business plan. Choose your roster size tier.

### Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, you can cancel anytime from your account settings. You will retain access until the end of your current billing period.

### What happens when my subscription expires?
You keep all your credits and profile data (these are free features). You lose access to Analytics, Catalog Audits, Discography Playlists, and Milestones.

### Do I get a refund if I cancel?
Refund requests are handled by our support team. Please create a support ticket and our team will review your request.

### How do I add more seats?
In your account settings under your subscription, you can add additional Pro seats at $7.50/month each.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit cards through our payment processor.

### I was charged but my subscription shows inactive
This can happen if there was a payment processing delay. Please create a support ticket with your payment confirmation and our team will resolve it.

## Self-Service Guide

Step-by-step instructions for the most common subscription actions.

### How to upgrade your plan
1. Log in at app.muso.ai
2. Go to Settings
3. Select your new plan (Pro or Business)
4. Choose billing period (monthly or annual) and confirm
5. Upgrade takes effect immediately

### How to cancel your subscription
1. Log in at app.muso.ai
2. Go to Settings
3. Find your current subscription and select Cancel
4. Confirm the cancellation
5. You keep access until the end of your current billing period
6. Your credits and profile data are never deleted – those are free features

### If you can't find the cancellation option
If the Cancel control isn't where you expect, the team can cancel it for you, so
the customer doesn't have to keep hunting for it. This usually comes up when:
- Settings shows no Billing or Subscription section
- The customer can't sign in to the account
- The subscription was set up through a third party (for example, an app store)
- The account email needs to be changed first

In any of these cases, ask the customer to send their account email and their
Muso profile link here in support, and the team will locate the subscription and
handle the cancellation.

### If a customer was charged after trying to cancel
First guide them to check Settings > Billing for the current subscription status.
If Apple or Google does not show the subscription, the cancel button is missing,
or the customer says they were charged after cancellation, collect the Muso
profile link, account email, charge date, amount, receipt screenshot, and whether
they purchased through web, Apple, or Google. Refund requests need team review
before any outcome is promised.

### How to update your payment method
1. Log in at app.muso.ai
2. Go to Settings > Billing
3. Update your card details
4. Save changes – new payment method applies to your next renewal

### How to check your renewal date
1. Log in at app.muso.ai
2. Go to Settings > Billing or Subscription
3. Your next renewal date is shown alongside your current plan

### What happens when your subscription expires
- You keep all credits and profile data (these are free, always)
- You lose access to: Analytics, Catalog Audits, Discography Playlists, Milestones
- For Business: you lose Company Analytics, List Analytics, Multi-User Workspace, Discovery Tools
- Your account stays active on the Lite plan – nothing is deleted

### How to add more seats (Pro)
1. Log in at app.muso.ai
2. Go to Settings > Subscription
3. Find the option to add Pro seats
4. Each additional seat costs $7.50/month
5. New seats are available immediately after adding
