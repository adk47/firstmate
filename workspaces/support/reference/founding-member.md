# Founding Member Program

The Founding Member program is a one-time recognition for customers who back
Muso.AI on a paid plan before **June 30, 2026**. After that date the group is
closed for good -- no future rounds, no backdating.

> This whole file is context for YOU. Answer customers naturally from it; never
> read it out, never name internal teams or owners, and never surface the items
> that are still being finalized.

## What members get

A Founding Member badge (a platinum plaque) on their profile, plus a perks
bundle:

- Founding Member **plaque / badge** on the profile (on Business, every seat in the org)
- **Monthly AI credits** included -- the exact amount is still being finalized, so never quote a number
- **Unlimited Connect & Chat** -- reach anyone in the industry
- **Priority support**
- **Early access** to new features and launches

There is **no pricing lock**. Do not promise that current pricing is fixed for
life, grandfathered, or locked in. If a customer says they were told their
pricing would be locked, don't confirm or deny it -- flag it for our team.

## Who's eligible

| Plan | Eligible? |
|---|---|
| Pro (any active paid variant) | Yes -- active paid only |
| Business (any active paid variant) | Yes -- covers every seat in the org |
| Trial (any tier) | No -- must be a paid plan |
| Lite / Free | No -- must upgrade to paid before June 30, 2026 |

Eligibility requires an **active, paid Pro or Business subscription before
June 30, 2026**. Trials and free/Lite plans don't qualify until they upgrade.

## How status works (the key rule)

Founding Member status holds **only while the subscription stays active** -- it's
tied to staying subscribed, not a permanent grant.

- If the subscription lapses, the member **loses** Founding Member status.
- There's roughly a **30-day grace window** for a lapse (e.g. a failed payment). Resolve payment inside that window and the status holds. Past the window, it's gone.
- **Lost is lost** -- once revoked it doesn't come back automatically. Any reinstatement needs our team's explicit approval; never promise to restore it in chat.
- Downgrading from a paid plan to Lite counts the same as cancelling -- the status lapses.

**Say it this way:** "as long as your subscription stays active", "while you're
subscribed", "you keep it by staying active".

**Never say:** "forever", "lifetime", "permanent regardless of plan", "locked in
for life". The continuous-subscription rule is the real one -- be warm but
straight about it, don't overpromise.

## Answering common questions

- **"How do I become one?"** Be on an active, paid Pro or Business plan before June 30, 2026. On Lite/free? Point them to the upgrade path and the deadline. After June 30: closed, no exceptions, no backdating.
- **"I upgraded -- where's my badge?"** Status applies while the paid subscription is active before the deadline; the badge shows on the profile. If it isn't showing, flag it for our team -- don't promise an ETA.
- **"If I cancel, do I lose it?"** Yes. It needs continuous subscription. Don't soften it.
- **"I cancelled last month -- can I get it back?"** No -- it's gone, and there's no automatic reinstatement. If they push, flag it for our team rather than promising anything.
- **"What perks do I get?"** List the five above. For credits, say "monthly AI credits included" and don't quote a number.
- **"Can I get it on Lite / free?"** No -- paid Pro or Business only. Offer the upgrade path.
- **"Will my pricing be locked in?"** No pricing lock, no grandfathering. If they insist they were promised it, flag it for our team.
- **"When does it close?"** June 30, 2026. After that it's permanently closed; no future rounds.
- **"Does my whole team get it on Business?"** Yes -- Business extends the badge to every seat, as long as the org's subscription stays active.
- **"What if I downgrade to Lite?"** Same as cancelling -- the status lapses.
- **"My payment failed by mistake?"** The ~30-day grace window applies; resolve it in time and the status holds. If a genuine billing or payment issue caused the lapse, flag it for our team -- don't auto-restore in chat.

## On-topic nudge (don't oversell)

When a Lite, free, or trial customer is already talking about plans, features, or
upgrading, it's fine to mention that upgrading to a paid Pro or Business plan
before June 30, 2026 makes them a Founding Member. Keep it light and relevant --
don't pitch it out of nowhere, and never recite the full perk list to someone
just because their account already shows founding-member status.

## When to flag for the team (don't improvise)

Flag for our team -- don't promise an outcome -- when:

- The badge isn't appearing or looks wrong on a profile.
- A lapse was caused by a billing or payment issue.
- Someone asks to reinstate lapsed status.
- A refund is tied to Founding Member status.
- A customer claims they were promised a pricing lock or lifetime rate.

## Hard rules

1. Never call the status "unconditional", "lifetime", or "permanent regardless of plan".
2. Never quote a specific monthly AI-credit number.
3. Never offer to reinstate a lapsed member -- that needs our team's approval.
4. Never promise a pricing lock or grandfathered rates.
5. "Priority support" is a listed perk, not a guaranteed response time -- never promise a specific faster ETA; flag issues normally.
6. The June 30, 2026 deadline is firm -- no backdating.
7. Flag edge cases for our team instead of improvising.
