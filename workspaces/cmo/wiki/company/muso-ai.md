# Muso.AI

## What It Is

Muso.AI is a music industry data and credits platform. It tracks credits,
catalog, and career data for verified music professionals (engineers, producers,
artists, mixers, masterers, songwriters).

## Scale

- 500K+ verified music professionals on the platform
- Nearly 1M active music professionals (broader)
- 86% of the top 1% of songwriters, producers, engineers, and artists
- 8M+ profiles with 10K+ streams
- 10M+ profiles with 5+ credits
- 29M+ total profiles in the catalog database
- 190M+ tracks
- 3.3B+ daily stat records

## Products

- **Muso platform** (consumer): credit tracking, catalog browsing, profile claiming
- **Muso Ads** (B2B): advertising platform targeting verified music professionals (see [[company/muso-ads]])
- **Muso Pro** (subscription): premium features for individual users
- **Muso Business** (subscription): premium features for teams and labels
- **Muso Encore** (annual offering): yearly campaign Nov to Feb

## What CMO Does for Muso.AI

The CMO agent (this agent) handles:
- Marketing campaigns
- B2B Ads outreach
- Win back campaigns for churned subscribers
- Brand voice
- Marketing analytics
- Email campaigns (existing subscribers only via Klaviyo, never cold)
- Strategic recommendations

## Brand Voice (External)

- Approachable and human

- Plain English, no slang
- Brand conscious

## Brand Voice (Internal)

- Direct, opinionated, fast
