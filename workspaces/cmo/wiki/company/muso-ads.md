# Muso Ads

## What It Is

Muso Ads is the B2B advertising platform built on top of Muso.AI's verified user
base. It lets music industry brands hyper target verified music professionals
by role, credits, genre, career stage, streams, and geography.

It is the only ad platform with this audience. Not available on Meta, Google,
or anywhere else.

## URL

- Platform: `access.muso.ai`
- Demo: `access.muso.ai/demo`
- Booking: `calendar.muso.ai`

## Key Selling Points

- **500K+ verified music professionals** (rising to nearly 1M active)
- **Hyper targeting** by:
  - Role (engineer, producer, songwriter, artist, etc)
  - Credits (number, recency, top artists worked with)
  - Genre
  - Career stage
  - Streams (lifetime, recent)
  - Geography
- **Specific subsegments**:
  - ~41K mixing engineers
  - ~272K US based professionals
  - ~36K UK based professionals
  - Credit recency targeting (last 90 days = actively working)
- **Not available anywhere else.** Completely unique inventory.

## Naming Rules

**This product is called "Muso Ads".** Other names that have been used:

- "Muso Access", old name, deprecated
- "B2B" / "B2B-Ads", internal shorthand only, never use externally
- "AMP", refers to the Muso Amp launch, not the ad platform

When writing externally, always use **Muso Ads**. See [[company/product-naming]].

## How It Compares to Internal Subscriptions

Do NOT confuse with:
- **Muso Business** (subscription tier for teams and labels)
- **Muso Business Pro** (premium subscription tier)

These are user subscriptions, not the ad platform. The ad platform sits at
`access.muso.ai`.
