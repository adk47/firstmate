# Product Naming Rules

## Authoritative Names

| Product | Correct Name | URL |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Consumer platform | Muso.AI | muso.ai |
| Ad platform | Muso Ads | access.muso.ai |
| Entry subscription | Muso Lite | muso.ai |
| Individual subscription | Muso Pro | muso.ai |
| Team subscription | Muso Business | muso.ai |
| Personalised career feed (in-app) | Amp (short for Amplify) | muso.ai |
| Annual yearly campaign | Muso Encore | muso.ai/encore |

_(corrected 2026-08-26: the row `| Premium team subscription | Muso Business Pro | muso.ai |`
was removed — **there is no "Business Pro" tier**. The tiers are **Lite / Pro / Business**.)_

## Forbidden Names

These are old or wrong. Never use externally.

| Wrong | Why It Is Wrong | Use Instead |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| Muso Access | Old name for the ad platform | Muso Ads |
| B2B | Internal shorthand only | Muso Ads |
| B2B-Ads | Internal shorthand only | Muso Ads |
| AMP | Amp is a shortening of **Amplify**, not an acronym — never all-caps | Amp (or Muso Ads, if you meant the ad platform) |
| AMPlify / Ad Measurement Platform / Accelerated Mobile Pages | None of these are what Amp stands for | Amp / Amplify |
| Business Pro | Not a tier. Never was. | Muso Business (the tiers are Lite / Pro / Business) |
| Pro | Ambiguous, could be Pro subscription or pro tier | Muso Pro (specific) |

_(corrected 2026-08-26: the `AMP` row previously read `| AMP | Refers to Muso Amp launch
(different thing) | Muso Ads |`, which taught the all-caps spelling it should have banned.)_

## Special Cases

### "Pro" disambiguation
- "Pro" alone is ambiguous. Always say `Muso Pro` (subscription) or `pro audio` (industry segment) explicitly.

### "Business" disambiguation
- `Muso Business` = team subscription tier
- The subscription tiers are **Lite / Pro / Business**. ~~`Muso Business Pro` = premium team subscription~~ — **corrected 2026-08-26: "Business Pro" does not exist.**
- Neither is the ad platform. The ad platform is `Muso Ads`.

### "Amp" vs "Muso Ads"
- `Amp` is short for **Amplify**, the personalised career feed inside the Muso app. It is
  **not an acronym** — never `AMP`, `AMPlify`, "Ad Measurement Platform", or "Accelerated
  Mobile Pages". Amp is user-side.
- `Muso Ads` is the advertising platform. Advertiser-side.
- These are NOT the same. Do not conflate.
- _(corrected 2026-08-26: this section was headed `"AMP" vs "Muso Ads"` and read
  `` `AMP` refers to Muso Amp, a separate product launch `` — right that they are different
  products, wrong about the spelling and about Amp being an acronym.)_

### "Encore"
- Annual yearly campaign that runs Nov to Feb
- December is the acquisition spike
- Jan to Feb is the churn wave (~35% of cancels)
- Compare year over year, not month over month

## Why This Matters

Past CMO/Clio agents called the ad platform "B2B" externally and confused customers.
Fede has explicitly directed: stop calling it B2B, use Muso Ads.

The agent's voice is the brand's voice. Wrong product names erode trust and
make Muso look disorganized.
