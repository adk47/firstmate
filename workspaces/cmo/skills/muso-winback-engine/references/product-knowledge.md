# What Muso Pro Unlocks

Use this to craft accurate win back pitches. **Get the gating right.** Past
versions made claims about features that turned out to be inaccurate, which
killed credibility on reply.

## Free for Everyone (Lite)

- Credit modifications (add/edit credits on your own profile)
- Basic profile page
- Audit summary view (you can SEE that gaps exist, but you can't drill in)
- Limited profile browsing (other artists)

## Pro Only

### Streaming Analytics
- Full streaming analytics across Spotify, YouTube, TikTok, Shazam, SoundCloud, Apple Music
- Per track, per platform, per region
- Real time refresh
- Historical trends

### Audit Reports
- Detailed audit reports
- Drill into specific missing credits
- Cross reference catalog against streaming services
- **MLC mechanical royalty audit** (the strongest single feature, see below)

### Roster Management
- Add profiles (any artist) to your Roster
- Track collaborators
- Workspace for teams (with Business plan)

### Discovery
- Add any profile to Roster
- See their analytics, collaborations, growth
- Track scout candidates

### Milestone Sharing
- Shareable achievement cards (certified streams, career firsts)
- Designed for social media

### Priority Data Capture
- Pro profiles refresh every 2 days
- Free profiles refresh every 45 to 180 days

## The MLC Angle (Strongest Pitch for Songwriters and Producers)

The Mechanical Licensing Collective (MLC) holds **unclaimed mechanical royalties**
for songwriters and producers who do not know they are owed money.

Muso's audit tool cross references a user's catalog against MLC data and flags
the gaps. For users with 500+ credits spanning songwriting or production, this
is **almost always worth a look** and is **the most compelling single pitch** in
the entire winback playbook.

When writing for songwriters or producers with high credit counts, lead with
this. Other features come second.

## What NOT to Claim

- Pro does NOT include direct payments or royalty collection (Muso flags the
  gaps, the user still has to claim through MLC)
- Pro does NOT include free profile claiming services (basic claiming is free)
- Pro does NOT include unlimited Discovery searches if there is a real cap
  (verify before claiming any "unlimited" feature)
- Pro does NOT include team workspace (that is Business plan)

If you are unsure about whether a feature is Pro or Business, ASK Fede or Aaron
before drafting.
