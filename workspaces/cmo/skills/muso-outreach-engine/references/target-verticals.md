# Target Verticals, Muso Ads

Updated 2026-09-15 for working creators, technical audio professionals,
independent artists, managers, labels, publishers, and rights operators.

## Priority 1 — Creator production and technical audio

- Audio plug-ins: mixing, mastering, instruments, FX, spatial/immersive audio
- Sample libraries, loop packs, sound-design tools, and sample subscriptions
- DAWs, DAW add-ons, cloud collaboration, versioning, and workflow utilities
- AI music tools: mastering, stems, MIDI/audio conversion, generation, vocal
  tuning, arrangement, and songwriting assistance
- Beat marketplaces, co-writing, collaboration, and producer marketplaces
- MIDI controllers, keyboards, drum machines, grooveboxes, and hybrid instruments
- Studio hardware: preamps, converters, monitors, microphones, and outboard gear
- Professional headphones, IEMs, and monitoring accessories
- Acoustic treatment, studio design, and build services
- Studio utilities: metering, restoration, calibration, loudness, and archiving

## Priority 2 — Rights, monetization, and artist growth

- Indie distribution and label services (exclude DistroKid-scale giants)
- Sync licensing libraries, music supervision tools, and sync agencies
- Royalty administration, publishing administration, neighboring rights, and
  mechanical licensing
- Royalty advances, music banking, catalog investment, and royalty marketplaces
- Catalog acquisition, valuation, management, and rights-data platforms
- Rights management, contract, split-sheet, cue-sheet, and copyright workflows
- Music PR, publicists, reputable playlist promotion, and release marketing
- Artist link-in-bio, EPK, fan CRM, direct-to-fan, and membership platforms
- Sponsorship and brand-partnership marketplaces for musicians
- Streaming, audience, release, and marketing analytics for independent artists
- Booking, touring, live-production, ticketing, merch fulfillment, and commerce

## Priority 3 — Education, community, and professional services

- Production, mix/master, songwriting, and music-business courses or masterclasses
- Co-write camps, songwriting/production contests, and creator communities
- Music-business schools, certificates, conferences, and NAMM/AES-adjacent events
- Entertainment lawyers and music-focused legal services
- Music accountants, business managers, tax, royalty, and financial advisory firms
- Music insurance, instrument insurance, and musician liability coverage
- Musician wellness, hearing protection, ergonomics, and occupational health

## Conditional / test-only

- Lifestyle brands only when active musician targeting is evidenced
- Fan monetization and music NFT platforms only when active, rights-compliant,
  and showing current marketing spend
- Online mixing/mastering services only when positioned for professionals or
  independent creators

## Deprioritized or excluded

- Generic creator SaaS without a music-professional use case
- Major platforms and giants with in-house media buying
- Products that compete directly with Muso
- Dormant/acquired crypto, NFT, or token projects without current evidence
- Unreputable playlisting, guaranteed-stream, or artificial-engagement services

## 2026 rationale

- NAMM's 2026 programming and show coverage emphasize AI workflow adoption,
  smart/hybrid instruments, immersive audio, and creator-led marketing.
- IAB projects US creator ad spend at $44B in 2026, supporting creator
  monetization and artist-growth categories.
- Spotify reports independent artists and labels accounted for half of its 2025
  royalty payouts; WIPO highlights growing music-rights investment and royalty
  marketplaces.
- AI licensing and attribution increase the relevance of rights-aware creation,
  metadata, royalty, and contract infrastructure.

## Usage

Priority 1 and 2 should generally outrank Priority 3 and conditional categories.
Every candidate still must pass the standard website, LinkedIn, newness,
liveness, size, market, spend, parent-company, and competitor checks.
