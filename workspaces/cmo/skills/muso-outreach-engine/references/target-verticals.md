# Target Verticals, Muso Ads

Active and exploration stage verticals for Muso Ads outreach. Use this to guide
target selection in the master sheet and in the daily company finder.

## Active Verticals

### Pro Audio / Gear
Their buyers ARE Muso's users (engineers, producers). Direct purchase intent.

### Music Software / Plugins
Same audience, contextual fit. Plugin ads next to production credits.

### Music Platforms
Want to acquire creators or listeners from a pre qualified music audience.

### Music Education
Subscription model, high CAC spend. Muso users are aspirational learners.

### Live / Touring / Ticketing
Reaching performing artists and their networks for event discovery.

### Industry Services (PROs, Labels)
Visibility with active professionals who deal with rights daily.

### Lifestyle / Adjacent
Headphone and culture brands that want credibility inside the music ecosystem.

## Exploration Verticals (Not Yet Pursued)

These are categories Muso has not yet approached but should explore.

### Entertainment / Music Lawyers
Firms specializing in sync, publishing, contracts. Muso users are the exact people
who need them.

### Royalty Collection / Admin
Songtrust, Sentric, Royalty Exchange. Users browsing credits care about getting
paid correctly.

### Sync Licensing
Musicbed, Artlist, Epidemic Sound. They are looking for composers and producers.
Muso profiles are basically resumes.

### Distribution
DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby, AWAL. Always acquiring independent artists.

### Music Insurance
Niche but real. Instrument and liability coverage marketed to working musicians.

### Merch / D2C
Printful, Bandzoogle, Shopify music vertical. Tools for monetizing fanbases.

## Usage

The daily company finder cron uses this list to guide discovery. When scoring
candidate companies, an active vertical match weights higher than an exploration
match, but exploration matches should still be surfaced and included in the
`#company-finder` batch for Fede's review.

## Keeping This Current

When Fede identifies a new vertical or retires one, update this file in place.
Add a `History` section at the bottom if the change is significant.
