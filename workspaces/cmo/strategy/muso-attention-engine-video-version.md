# MUSO ATTENTION ENGINE — Strategy Overview

## The Opportunity

Muso.AI has built something nobody else has: 29.4 million verified music professional profiles. 357,000 registered users. 14,500 active subscribers paying for access. $192K in monthly recurring revenue. The world's largest verified music credits database.

But we're sitting on a much bigger opportunity.

Right now, Muso is a credits platform — people come to check their credits, verify their work, and track their streaming stats. It works. But it's utility. Check and leave.

What if Muso became the place where music professionals connect, discover each other, and do business — every single day?

---

## The Proof It Works

In December 2025, we ran an experiment. We sent two different emails to our free user base:

Email A told people: "Someone's been viewing your Muso profile. See who →"
Email B was our strongest seasonal promotion — discounts, urgency, limited time.

Results:

- Profile View email: 78.8% open rate, 11.0% click-through rate, 12,794 people clicked
- Seasonal promotion: 59.4% open rate, 1.6% click-through rate, 347 clicks

The Profile View email outperformed our best campaign by 6.8x on click rate. For context, industry average email open rate is 21%. Ours was 78.8% — nearly 4x the industry.

We ran it twice to be sure. The numbers actually got better at scale.

The signal is unmistakable: music professionals desperately want to know who's paying attention to their career.

---

## The Platform Evolution

### Profile Views — The Daily Hook

LinkedIn's most addictive feature isn't job listings. It's "47 people viewed your profile this week."

We're building this for music. Notifications via email and push, daily. See who's been looking at your credits, your catalog, your profile. The top viewers shown by popularity — the rest available to Pro subscribers.

This turns a monthly check-in into a daily habit.

The engineering foundation is already in place — ADR-029 approved, implementation has started, design research and Figma prototypes are complete.

With our email list of 367,000 people and proven 11% click-through rates on profile view notifications, even a conservative 5% daily click-through would add 18,000+ daily sessions. That could double our baseline daily active users.

### Chat — The Connection Layer

Profile Views gets people to the app. Chat gives them a reason to stay.

The flow: you get a notification that someone viewed your profile → you open the app → you see they're an engineer who worked on an album you admire → you message them → they respond → now you're having a real conversation about music.

Nobody else offers verified music professionals a way to connect based on real, verified credits. On LinkedIn, you message someone because of their job title. On Muso, you message someone because they played bass on the record that changed your life. That's fundamentally more meaningful.

The vision extends further: sync supervisors finding artists for placements. Labels discovering talent. Session musicians finding gigs. Managers finding collaborators. All happening organically on the platform, driven by verified professional credibility.

### Onboarding — The Foundation

For the engagement loop to work, profiles need to be real — name, role, genre, photo. We're implementing a mandatory completion flow so every new user builds a profile worth viewing, messaging, and connecting with.

## The Growth Plan — 90 Days

### Month 1: Ship the Hook
- Launch Profile Views with premium gating (free users see that people viewed them, Pro users see who)
- Launch Chat MVP (direct messaging from profile pages)
- Ship improved onboarding (mandatory profile completion)
- Target: daily active users from 13K baseline → 25K+

### Month 2: Amplify
- Paid acquisition campaign with new positioning: "The professional network for music"
- Influencer seeding: custom Muso plaques to 30 high-profile professionals already on the platform
- Full email reactivation: 367K list blast with profile view notifications
- Viral video campaign showcasing the new connected Muso experience
- Target: monthly active users from 70K → 120K+

### Month 3: Monetize the Attention
- With 2 months of real engagement data, pitch advertisers with honest, verifiable daily active user metrics
- Muso Ads: the only ad platform targeting verified music professionals by role, credits, genre, career stage, and geography
- Updated B2B outreach with case studies from existing ad partners
- Target: 3-5 new advertiser deals, MRR from $192K → $210K+
