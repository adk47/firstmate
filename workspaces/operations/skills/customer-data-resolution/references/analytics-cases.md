# Analytics cases

Part of the `customer-data-resolution` skill. The safety rules in its `SKILL.md` bind every case here and win over anything in this file.

Read before replying to an analytics discrepancy (SKILL.md Analytics discrepancy safety rule).

## TikTok short links

Follow each short link to its final sound page and compare the sound IDs.

## Row-level popularity/display metadata

Aggregate totals, cube readiness, and stream history are not resolution proof. Read every named track’s served fields and require an exact pass count (for example, 4/4) before replying.

## Inflated YouTube source

For an inflated YouTube source, compare the stored metric with the live platform count and published date; if the stored digits encode the date rather than views, preserve the valid source, route correction of that metric plus history rebuild, and require source-level, track-total, and profile-total read-back.
