For each credit, any **one** document is enough. These work well:
- **Mixing / mastering:** the invoice or payment record for the track (you can black out amounts), the work order, or a screenshot of the delivered session or master with the track title and dates.
- **Producer:** the producer agreement or letter of direction that names the track, a royalty or advance statement listing it, or the DAW project with the track title and dates.
- **Songwriter / composer:** the split sheet, or your PRO or MLC registration for the song.
- **Engineer / assistant:** the session invoice, the studio booking, or an email from the artist, label or studio naming you on the session.
- **Musician / vocals:** the session invoice or AFM/union report, or an email from the artist, producer or label confirming your part.

One document can cover several credits when it names all of those tracks, for example an album split sheet or an invoice for a whole project.

Whenever it suits you, reply to this message with the files (PDF, PNG or JPG). We check every document and aim to get back to you within 14 days of receiving it.

Thanks for helping us keep credits accurate. It protects your work too.
