# House wiki
Muso house knowledge lives at `~/.llm-wiki` and is the source of truth for Muso goals, infra, memories, and apps.
Grep `wiki/index.md` then Read the matching page before acting on a Muso fact.
Grep `raw/sources/*/extracted.md` for identifiers (PR URLs, job ids, image tags); read `wiki/` for meaning. `wiki-find` searches both.
Pi uses `wiki_recall`. File a lesson with `wiki-retro` (Pi: `wiki_retro`) when you learn something durable.
Never claim a fact is absent from memory without grepping `~/.llm-wiki` first.
Never edit `raw/` or `meta/`; never call `/wiki-run` or `wiki_watch`.
Do not add MCP for this vault.
